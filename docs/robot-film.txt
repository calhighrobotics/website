1516A BACKGROUND FILM — CAMERA CUTS

The robot is stationary. The camera moves at a uniform angular speed within
each shot, then cuts immediately to a different viewpoint. There is no
turntable animation, easing, reversing, crossfade, or cursor response.

Delivery: silent H.264 MP4, 1920x1080, 24 frames per second, 12 seconds.
Three shots of 96 frames each. Cuts occur at 4 and 8 seconds; looping at
12 seconds is another intentional hard cut.

Camera paths:
- Low front three-quarter: starts at 35 degrees azimuth, 14 degrees elevation.
- Opposite side: starts at 135 degrees azimuth, 27 degrees elevation.
- High rear three-quarter: starts at 245 degrees azimuth, 42 degrees elevation.
- Every shot: fixed target and 2-metre radius, 3 degrees/second azimuth motion.
- Every frame advances azimuth by 0.125 degrees. There are no repeated or
  reversed frames in the source sequence.

The corrected original club CAD/material scene is used. Its 990 visible bodies
keep their transforms and mechanism state. Matte rubber rollers, brushed
aluminum, and the corrected Brain display orientation are retained. The
original Blender file is not overwritten.

The film sits behind the team page's dark gradient. Pause/resume, offscreen
pausing, reduced-motion and data-saving defaults remain available. The optional
3D explorer changes view only through deliberate controls.
